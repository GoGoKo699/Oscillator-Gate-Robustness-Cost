## Current result

For independent forces in a specified finite nominally closed space, the minimum integrated squared effective force for first-order static angle robustness is |Theta0|/min_zeta ||K-zeta D||. It is attained by an explicitly constructed pair. Balanced extreme eigenvalues of K are necessary and sufficient for no additional integrated-angle cost. Conjugation-symmetric controls guarantee that balance and a stronger all-waveform phase-sign symmetry. A matched four-positive-tone space has a strictly unavoidable cost factor 5.213046..., while the symmetric ±1,±2 space has factor one.

The consolidation makes its gate meaning explicit. The exact quadratic average-infidelity coefficient is a sum of the angle-slope square and the two residual-displacement-derivative squares. Projecting the original control space onto its first-moment kernel, then solving the same scalar problem, therefore gives the exact minimum for O(delta^4) full-gate infidelity with any fixed finite thermal oscillator. A lower force budget in a costly space has a positive, computable quadratic-infidelity floor. This is a corollary and an interpretation, not a new noise model or a solved finite-error operating-interval optimization.

## Strongest implication-level comparison

**The optimization mathematics has an explicit predecessor.** The equality S-lemma of Xia, Wang and Sheu, in its homogeneous sign-indefinite case, recovers the scalar norm formula immediately after the x,y pulse transformation. THEOREM.md gives the exact quadratic forms and block matrix. The reference does not need to mention oscillator gates for that mathematical implication to hold. We must not claim a new minimax theorem, convexity principle, or generic quadratically constrained optimizer.

**The physical optimization ingredients are inherited too.** Blümel et al.'s S16 Eqs. S82–S83 already impose bilinear target-angle and sensitivity constraints for independently applied pulses. The subsequent explicit recipe freezes a nominally power-optimal first pulse and projects the second. Their broader problem involves physical motional modes, real laboratory functions, multiple constraints and higher orders; our exact scalar solution is a single positive-overlap-constraint specialization, not a solution or refutation of that general problem. The old 4.664 factor is our adapted, fixed-first construction penalty in one symmetric effective space, not their device's measured power ratio.

**The pulse primitive is not new.** Spiller et al. Sec. IV already uses orthogonal-quadrature controlled displacements and returns the bus without measurement. Jia et al. already cancels angle sensitivity by a different reflected-frequency, multimode construction. Displacement moments are established pulse-design tools. The new joint error-generator and fidelity formulas use standard unitary perturbation theory, Weyl relations and Nielsen's average-fidelity identity.

The strongest candidate statement is therefore not 'a new robust gate' or 'a new optimizer.' It is **an exact resource classification for a defined physical control class, distinguishing a penalty enforced by the allowed pulses from a penalty introduced by the chosen construction, with attainable certificates and full-gate implications**.

Consolidation is justified; it is not a strong PRL acceptance forecast.

The strongest significance limitation remains the single-mode, single-static-error, integrated-force setting. Familiar mathematical machinery is not itself a reason to reject a physical result, and successful check suites are not evidence of broad significance.

The next workspace should review the scoped scientific account and its exact attribution, not automatically add heating, spectator modes, individual peak optimization or arbitrary higher-order robustness.
