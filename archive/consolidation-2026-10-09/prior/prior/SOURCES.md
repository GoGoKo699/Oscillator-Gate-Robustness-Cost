# Primary-source comparison record

Read 9 October 2026. No third-party PDF is redistributed. The archived pilot has its own preserved source log.

## BG21

R. Blümel, N. Grzesiak, N. Pisenti, K. Wright and Y. Nam, *Power-optimal, stabilized entangling gate between trapped-ion qubits*, npj Quantum Information 7, 147 (2021), arXiv:1905.09292v2; DOI 10.1038/s41534-021-00489-w.

Read the primary combined PDF's Hamiltonian and pulse definition; S4's projected quadratic optimization; S15's single-pulse derivative obstruction; S16 Eqs. S82–S83 and its fixed-first-pulse projection recipe; S17; and S19's real multitone laboratory synthesis. The publisher's main text and Methods were cross-checked. The reported increased-power examples are not a universal theorem for arbitrary complex one-mode control spaces. Our 4.664 cost ratio is newly calculated after transferring the algebraic recipe to our explicit space, not extracted from their graphs. All source pulse normalizations are kept separate from our Theta convention; overall factors do not affect the adapted ratio.

S7 states a common-pulse nominal optimum. Our resource proof uses the general spectral bound rather than claiming identical pulses are the only possible optimum: conjugation supplies equal-magnitude opposite eigenvalues, so additional minimizing pairs can exist. We do not claim a defect in the source's multimode implementation from that observation.

## Q06

T. P. Spiller et al., *Quantum Computation by Communication*, New Journal of Physics 8, 30 (2006), arXiv:quant-ph/0509202v3.

Read Section IV, Eqs. 7–9 and the surrounding control description. Orthogonal-quadrature conditional displacements and bus-disentangling geometric gates are inherited. Applying our stipulated detuning perturbation to that control pattern gives the phase-sign symmetry; this is a derivation, not a quoted theorem in the source.

## I24

Y.-H. Hou et al., *Individually addressed entangling gates in a two-dimensional ion crystal*, Nature Communications 15, 9710 (2024); DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-53405-z.

Read the publisher Methods, “Phase-modulated gate design.” It explicitly distinguishes motional and spin phases and uses rotating-wave approximations. Used to identify relevant independent phase controls, not to claim this device realizes our simultaneous four-tone waveforms or our error budget.
